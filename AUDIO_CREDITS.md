# Recorded engine audio credits

The engine beds in this game are streamed from the original Ogg Opus recordings listed below. The game changes the playback rate to follow simulated RPM and loops each source recording while driving. No synthetic engine oscillators or synthesized exhaust harmonics are used.

All six Commons file pages identify the recording as **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)**. Credit is retained here and in the vehicle audio profiles. The Commons pages also currently display a **“License review needed (audio)”** notice, meaning the upload's license has not been independently confirmed by a Commons reviewer. Re-check the source page before distributing a build beyond this project.

## Important accuracy note

A verified, reusable Senna-specific engine recording was not located in the available sources. The Senna currently uses a clearly labelled McLaren 720S recording as a temporary engine-family proxy; it is **not** represented as an exact Senna recording. Replace that profile's \`sampleUrl\` and attribution fields once a Senna recording with verifiable reuse rights is supplied.

The in-game sample playback rate is adjusted dynamically and the source recordings are looped. These are real recordings, but the loop boundaries and playback-rate ranges are approximations rather than a professionally authored multi-RPM sample bank.
